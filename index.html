THE FIELD GUIDE

Make a life in
Willowbrook.

This is your farm, at your pace. Earn experience by caring for animals, harvesting clover and helping the herd grow. Processing a cow is always optional.

01 / Explore

WASD or arrows to walk. Click or tap the ground to travel. Select an animal or a golden garden plot to see its actions.

02 / Nurture

Feed and water your animals. Mara helps those most in need. Eli and your dog watch for wolves. Harvest clover, then sell it or turn it into feed in the General Store.

03 / Grow

Invite B.O.V.I. to a healthy adult cow for 25 coins. The robot performs artificial insemination, starting a 100-second pregnancy. Cows and reserved calf slots share a limit of 20.

04 / Discover

Levels unlock rain, frost, growth and fire magic. Keys 1–4 cast spells. Each spell has its own mana cost and cooldown.

05 / Settle

Buy seeds, cottages, barns and walls in the General Store. Choose a structure, then click open ground to place it. Escape cancels. A larger settlement attracts more wolves; equip weapons and press F or Attack to defend it.

06 / Belong

Build a cottage to invite an adult companion. Shared meals grow your bond. At level 3 and bond 100, you can begin an optional family chapter.

Engineering: buy a Voltwarden for electric defense. It has 240 armor and stays repairable when offline. Buy an Insulated screwdriver, select a damaged guardian and stay close to repair for 6 gold/min. A hired technician repairs twice as fast for 12 gold/min; select them to enable or pause maintenance. Only active repair time is charged. No fixed classes: freely combine farming, magic and engineering.

Your farm saves locally every five seconds. Time stops while paused or away. Sound starts only when you choose it.

YOUR FARM, YOUR CHOICE

Send this cow to market?

This optional, non-graphic route permanently removes the selected adult cow from your herd and rewards 70 coins and 25 XP. You can progress entirely through care instead.
